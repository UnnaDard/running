Race Day Checklist

UNLOCKS IN TAPER WEEK · OCT 5
Lay out kit the night beforeShoes, socks, shorts, singlet, watch charged. Nothing new on race day.
Bib pinned, timing chip attachedDo it the night before, not in the car park.
Breakfast 2–3 hours before startSomething you've eaten before a run already. Toast, banana, coffee.
Hydrate early, taper off 45min outSip, don't flood. You don't want a full bladder on the start line.
Arrive 45min earlyToilet queues are longer than you think. Build in slack.
10min warm-up jog + stridesFinish the warm-up about 10 minutes before the gun.
Pacing plan: first 2k feel too easyTarget 6:00–6:15/km. Going out hot is the classic 10k mistake.
Last 2k — empty the tankYou've done the work. This is the part you get to enjoy.

Before you push harder

Soreness is still present, not resolved. If it sharpens or lingers past 48 hours on any session, swap that day for rest — don't run through it.

Every "easy" session stays conversational. The speed and tempo days are your only hard efforts — that contrast is what makes the plan work.

Week 3 is a deliberate down week. Trust it — it's what lets Weeks 4 and 5 land.
